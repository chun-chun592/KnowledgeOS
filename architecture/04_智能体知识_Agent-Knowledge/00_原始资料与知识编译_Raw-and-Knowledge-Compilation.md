# 原始资料与知识编译 / Raw Sources and Knowledge Compilation

raw/documents、papers 与 websites 保持原始证据。将授权来源按原件保留并记录可追溯路径和哈希。Wiki 保存可更新的知识结论，Schema 决定结构，Index 提供导航，Log 记录演进。\
Raw documents, papers, and websites hold original evidence. Retain approved originals and record their traceable paths and hashes. The wiki contains maintained knowledge, the schema defines structure, the index provides navigation, and the log records evolution.

Concept 解释概念，Entity 描述对象，Topic 组织主题，Comparison 比较选项，Synthesis 综合证据，Project 跟踪项目，Decision 保留决策依据。Source 页记录原件与出处，可放在 Wiki 根；实际目录见[知识目录](../02_知识架构_Knowledge-Architecture/00_知识目录_Knowledge-Layout.md)。\
Concept explains an idea, Entity describes a subject, Topic organizes an area, Comparison compares options, Synthesis combines evidence, Project tracks work, and Decision records rationale. A Source note traces an original and can live at the wiki root. See the [knowledge tree](../02_知识架构_Knowledge-Architecture/00_知识目录_Knowledge-Layout.md).

摄取和综合先查重，优先更新原页。区分事实、推断及意见，保留相互矛盾的证据及适用范围；过期内容标为 outdated，待核验标为 needs-review。每个知识页应能沿来源与相关链接复用。\
Intake and synthesis check for overlap before drafting and prefer the existing page. Separate findings, inferences, and opinions. Preserve contradictory evidence with its scope, mark stale material outdated and uncertain material needs-review. Each page should be reusable through source and related links.
