# Human 目录与知识地图 / Human Directories and Maps

Inbox 暂存，Daily 捕获日常，Projects 管有期限的成果，Areas 管持续责任。Resources 保存有复用价值的材料，Archive 保存告一段落的内容。book 与 Clippings 为保留的已有分类，Attachments 保存附件。\
Inbox captures incoming material; Daily records ongoing work. Projects track outcomes and Areas track ongoing responsibilities. Resources hold reusable material, Archive holds inactive content, book and Clippings preserve existing categories, and Attachments hold supporting files.

Resources 中 Literature 保留阅读证据，Concepts 与 Permanent-Notes 连接理解及独立结论，References 保存导航和来源。MOCs 是人工组织的主题地图，可以从已有链接自下而上形成。\
Within Resources, Literature keeps reading evidence, Concepts and Permanent-Notes connect understanding and standalone conclusions, and References hold navigation and sources. MOCs are curated maps that can grow bottom-up from existing links.

Human 用任意 Markdown 工具管理自己的内容，可选用 [Human 模板](../../vault-template/en-US/30_System/templates/human-note.md)。Human 不受 Agent Schema 或编译流水线约束。Agent 只能在获准范围内读取、引用和交付提案。\
Humans manage their content with any Markdown editor and may use the [Human template](../../vault-template/en-US/30_System/templates/human-note.md). Agent schemas and compilation rules do not govern Human notes. Agents read and link only within approved scopes and deliver proposals for Human changes.

See the [knowledge tree](../02_知识架构_Knowledge-Architecture/00_知识目录_Knowledge-Layout.md) for the complete structural layout and retention boundary.
