# Human Knowledge Index

Use this page to organize active projects, ongoing areas, resource topics, and maps of content.

## Organize personal knowledge

Use Inbox for unprocessed notes, Daily for dated notes, Projects for time-bounded work and Areas for ongoing responsibilities. Resources holds concept, reading, permanent and reference notes. Use MOCs to curate topic maps, Attachments for media, book for book material, Clippings for captured excerpts and Archive for inactive material. Link relevant notes from this index as your organization evolves.
