# Vault entry point

Read the [permissions](30_System_知识库管理/policies_管理规则/ownership-and-permissions_归属与权限.md) and [routing](30_System_知识库管理/workflows_知识处理流程/knowledge-routing_知识工作流程.md), then choose relevant Skills and available tools.
